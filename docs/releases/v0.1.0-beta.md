# PaperLens v0.1.0-beta

PaperLens public beta turns supported arXiv papers into a visual reader with
source-linked evidence. The release includes structured document normalization,
evidence registry navigation, optional evidence-grounded extraction and claim
verification, Paper Chat, workspaces, bounded cross-paper research runs, and
lexical BM25 retrieval.

## Supported inputs

Only arXiv identifiers and arXiv URLs are supported in this release. The beta
does not claim IEEE, DOI, arbitrary publisher PDF, or user-upload ingestion.

## Availability and safety

AI analysis, semantic retrieval, Paper Chat, and the Research Agent are exposed
only when deployment capability flags and provider credentials enable them.
Without live AI credentials, ingestion, reader, evidence, source PDF viewing,
workspaces, and BM25 remain available and AI controls are disabled cleanly.

## Limitations

The Phase 10 benchmark is `PRELIMINARY`, not a validated scientific accuracy
claim. Metrics and rate limits are process-local, figure crops and PDF region
highlighting are limited, and the beta has no accounts or collaboration.
Important conclusions must be checked against the original source.
